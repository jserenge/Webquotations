## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tension between architectural restraint and tactile warmth, engineered for an elite lifestyle e-commerce imprint. Rooted in structural minimalism and editorial print design, the digital environment behaves like a high-end gallery space: deliberate, silent, unhurried, and prioritizing object presentation over decorative interface chrome.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Curated Silence:** The interface avoids visual noise, allowing imagery, typography, and negative space to define status.
- **Architectural Precision:** Every line, grid boundary, and typographic alignment communicates purpose and industrial refinement.
- **Understated Intimacy:** Micro-interactions are brief and damped, delivering feedback through fine hairline shifts and soft tonal washes rather than bouncy, playful animations.

### Visual Style
- **High-End Editorial Minimalism:** High-density spatial architecture balanced by generous outer canvas margins.
- **Hairline Construction:** Divisions are articulated via microscopic 1px borders, subtle surface value transitions, and strict typographic hierarchy rather than heavy drop shadows or bright badges.
- **Art Direction:** Visual assets utilize muted, naturalistic grading, direct-angle focal lengths, and deliberate crop geometries that integrate seamlessly with the porcelain foundation.

## Colors

The chromatic system operates on low-saturation, ceramic-inspired neutrals designed to fade into the background so product imagery and typography take primacy.

### Palette Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#1A1A1A`):** The stark charcoal ink applied to all structural typography, primary buttons, high-priority state markers, and active interaction states.
- **Secondary (`#706E6A`):** A warm geological gray utilized for meta annotations, subtitles, subtle active states, product specifications, and breadcrumb trails.
- **Tertiary (`#C4A480`):** A restrained raw-linen accent reserved strictly for editorial callouts, rare material tags, stock scarcity indicators, and delicate accent touches.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBFBFA`):** A luminous porcelain base calibrated with faint warm stone undertones to avoid the harsh sterility of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Surface Elevation Tint (`#F7F7F6`):** Secondary paneling, drawer panels, product thumbnail backdrops, and active row indicators.
- **Hairline Outlines (`#E5E5E3` / `#ECECEB`):** Structural 1px division rules, container borders, and quiet functional split lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes structural cleanliness and editorial legibility, deploying **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly to create a monolithic visual identity.

### Structural Conventions
- **Light Display Scales:** Large display tiers (`display-xl`, `display-lg`) are set at weight `300` with negative tracking. This provides large headlines with a refined, architectural quality without feeling heavy or aggressive.
- **Controlled Text Scaffolding:** Body copy relies on strict optical line-height ratios (1.5x–1.6x) to preserve whitespace between lines of description and editorial essays.
- **Tracked Technical Labels:** Small functional labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) use uppercase rendering with open letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to replicate the precision of luxury Swiss packaging, archive labels, and museum plaques.
- **Numeral Handling:** Numbers for pricing and inventory indicators use tabular figures to maintain vertical baseline rhythm down checkout columns and specification tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a fluid 12-column architectural grid defined by high-contrast negative margins and crisp internal division lines.

### Grid Rhythm & Viewports
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `margin-lg` (5rem) margins, and `gutter-lg` (2.5rem) gutters. Product grids typically occupy 3-column spans (4 items per row) or 6-column editorial spans (alternating full-bleed imagery and prose blocks).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin` (3rem) margins, and `gutter` (1.5rem) gutters. Product feeds collapse into 4-column spans (2 items per row).
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1.25rem) margins, and `gutter-sm` (1rem) gutters. Grids collapse into full-width or single-column carousels with edge snap alignment.

### Spatial Spacing Principles
- Vertical page sections observe generous pacing: hero compositions and editorial stories separate with `space-xl` multipliers (4.5rem to 8rem).
- Component clusters (such as color swatches, size selectors, and purchase buttons) sit within tight structural modules bound by `space-sm` and `space-md`, establishing clear groupings against expansive canvas borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects physical drop shadows and heavy multi-tiered elevations. Visual hierarchy is achieved exclusively through **tonal surfaces**, **1px hairline divisions**, and **deliberate spatial layering**.

### Elevation Strategies
- **Surface Layering:** Base page containers sit on `#FBFBFA`. Interactive panels, secondary modules, and cart slide-outs use `#F7F7F6` with hairline border containment (`#E5E5E3`), creating a quiet sense of physical sheets of fine art paper laid on top of one another.
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth between layout regions is articulated by crisp `1px solid #E5E5E3` lines. Elements never float detached; they anchor to structural grid axes.
- **Modal & Backdrop Handling:** Full-screen overlays and modal backdrops utilize `#1A1A1A` with a restrained 24% opacity combined with a subtle backdrop filter blur (`4px`), muting background noise while keeping the focal product layer anchored to the page.
- **Hover Depth:** Interactive elevations avoid upward displacement or growing drop shadows. Interactive states signal feedback via hairline color shifts (`#E5E5E3` to `#1A1A1A`) and subtle background color transitions over 200ms ease-out curves.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly architectural and uncompromising: **Sharp (`0px` border-radius)** across all interface primitives.

### Geometric Language
- **Crisp Rectilinear Form:** Buttons, inputs, product presentation cards, contextual overlays, and modal dialogues feature zero corner rounding. This creates a gallery-grade visual language reminiscent of bespoke architectural facades, modern typography journals, and physical luxury packaging.
- **Exceptions:** Functional system status dots (such as active swatch indicators or live inventory signals) maintain a pure circular ratio (1:1 with 50% border radius) to contrast intentionally against the rigid rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A1A1A`, text `#FBFBFA`, sharp corners, 0px border. Padding: 1rem horizontal, 1.25rem vertical. Typography: `label-md` (uppercase, letter-spaced). Hover state: background transition to `#2B2D2F` over 180ms ease.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px hairline border `#1A1A1A`, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover state: background fills `#1A1A1A`, text converts to `#FBFBFA`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Background transparent, text `#1A1A1A`, no border, trailing 1px hairline bottom underline with a 4px gap. Hover: underline extends smoothly from left to right.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural baseline or full-box enclosure using 1px hairline `#E5E5E3`. Background `#FBFBFA` or `#FFFFFF`.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#1A1A1A`. Placeholder text in `#706E6A`.
- Active / Focus: Border shifts to `#1A1A1A` with no secondary outer ring. Error state: border color `#9E3B3B` with micro-annotation in `body-sm` beneath.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Sharp rectangular containers free of shadows or exterior outlines. 
- Image containers sit on a `#F7F7F6` background with `overflow: hidden` and an intentional 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratio. Hover induces a gentle image scale (`scale(1.02)`) over 400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).
- Card metadata: Item title in `headline-sm`, category in `label-sm` (uppercase, `#706E6A`), price in `body-md` (tabular numerals).

### Chips & Attribute Selectors
- Flat rectangular tiles bounded by 1px hairline `#E5E5E3`. Internal padding `0.5rem 1rem`.
- Inactive: Text `#706E6A`, background transparent.
- Active / Selected: Background `#1A1A1A`, text `#FBFBFA`, border `#1A1A1A`.
- Unavailable / Out of Stock: Strikethrough diagonal hairline divider across the rectangle with opacity reduced to 40%.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: 16px × 16px square, 1px border `#1A1A1A`, zero corner radius. Checked: Solid `#1A1A1A` fill with a crisp white hairline checkmark.
- Radios: 16px × 16px outer square (or circle for swatches), unselected border `#E5E5E3`. Selected: 1px hairline `#1A1A1A` border enclosing an 8px solid center dot.

### Lists & Specifications
- Clean horizontal rows separated by 1px solid `#ECECEB`.
- Attributes structured into two-column rows: left column set in `label-md` uppercase (`#706E6A`), right column set in `body-md` (`#1A1A1A`).

### Cart Drawer / Slide-Over Panel
- Fixed panel anchoring to the right edge with a sharp 1px left border in `#E5E5E3`. Background `#FBFBFA`.
- Sticky footer containing line-item calculation breakdowns separated by hairline rules, culminating in a full-width primary checkout trigger.
