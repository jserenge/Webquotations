## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a high-end editorial wellness and culinary philosophy. It marries the disciplined tranquility of architectural minimalism with the rich, grounded warmth of botanical living. The target audience comprises discerning health-conscious professionals, epicureans, and wellness practitioners seeking clinical nutritional rigor presented with magazine-grade elegance.

The emotional signature is grounding, opulent, and restorative. Rather than relying on sterile, hyper-clinical whites or clinical blues, the visual direction embraces tactile warmth—pairing deep, restorative forest tones with layered, unbleached stone and cream surfaces. Generous structural whitespace, deliberate typographic scale shifts, and quiet micro-interactions imbue the interface with the timeless poise of an architectural monograph or luxury gastronomic journal.

## Colors

The palette draws its strength from high-density botanical foliage offset against soft, organic mineral surfaces.

- **Primary (`#12372A`)**: Deep Jungle Green anchors primary navigation, high-contrast actions, key editorial headings, and prominent brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F`)**: Botanical Green provides functional accents, active segment fills, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Soft Champagne Gold introduces measured warmth for highlights, achievement states, ratings, and delicate accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5`)**: Warm Ivory serves as the baseline background to eliminate clinical glare and establish an analog paper quality.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchy
- **Surfaces & Tonal Backdrops**:
  - `surface-base`: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Ivory)
  - `surface-raised`: `#F4EFEA` (Soft Cream)
  - `surface-recessed`: `#E8E3DC` (Natural Stone Neutral)
  - `surface-inverse`: `#12372A` (Deep Jungle Green)
- **Text & Editorial Ink**:
  - `text-primary`: `#111815` (Charcoal Forest)
  - `text-secondary`: `#1B4332` (Rich Forest Green)
  - `text-muted`: `#637069` (Muted Botanical Slate)
  - `text-inverse`: `#FBF9F5`
- **Functional Accents**:
  - `accent-warm`: `#D97706` (Warm Amber for alerts, metabolic warnings, and energy cues)
  - `accent-earth`: `#C86D51` (Muted Terracotta for seasonal dietary markers and tag accents)
- **Borders & Rules**:
  - `border-hairline`: `#E8E3DC`
  - `border-subtle`: `#DFD7CC`

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances the high-contrast drama of a bespoke culinary publication with the razor-sharp legibility required for biometric data and nutritional metrics.

- **Headlines & Feature Titles**: Set in `Playfair Display`. It provides sculptural curves, deep serifs, and editorial authority. It should be used for cover lines, dish names, nutritional philosophy headings, and hero narratives. Avoid setting all-caps in this face.
- **Body & Data Displays**: Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its geometric clarity, open counters, and modern proportions maintain supreme readability across meal plans, macronutrient breakdowns, and long-form dietary guidance.
- **Labels, Metadata, & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with relaxed letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) at sizes between 11px and 13px, instilling an architectural indexing quality.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts in this design system follow an asymmetrical, editorial rhythm inspired by curated print layouts. Rather than uniform modular blocks, the structure alternates between expansive full-bleed imagery, staggered multi-column grids, and generous breathing room.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with `gutter-desktop` (40px) and generous canvas margins (`margin-desktop`: 64px, scaling up to max-width containers of 1440px). Columns allow wide editorial recipes (8 cols) paired with contextual nutrition sidebars (4 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Multi-column cards compress into alternating 4-column pairs.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px margins (`margin`). Staggered editorial cards collapse to stacked vertical arrangements.

### Spacing Principles
- Component inner paddings use `space-md` (16px) for compact widgets, shifting to `space-lg` (24px) or `space-xl` (40px) for elevated cards.
- Layout sections require substantial vertical clearance (minimum 64px on mobile, 96px to 128px on desktop) to preserve the brand's quiet, unhurried presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate tonal transitions and diffused, natural light rather than harsh drop shadows. The aesthetic mimics fine matte art paper laid over warm stone.

### Shadow Character
- **Subtle Surface (Cards & Modules)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 55, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 55, 42, 0.02)`. Tinted exclusively with `#12372A` to maintain chromatic harmony with the botanical palette.
- **Raised Interactive (Hovered cards, dropdown menus)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 55, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(18, 55, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Modals, quick-view culinary sheets)**: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(17, 24, 21, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(17, 24, 21, 0.04)`.

### Borders & Divisors
Elements are grounded through structural 1px hairline rules rendered in `#E8E3DC` or `#DFD7CC`. Outlines are consistently crisp, framing full-bleed imagery and separating nutrition tables with surgical, catalog-like precision.

### Backdrops
Modals and persistent navigation apply a subtle translucent frosted layer: `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` accompanied by a `16px` backdrop blur, permitting underlying culinary textures to bleed softly through.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a hybrid geometric balance: architectural discipline meets soft organic containment.

- **Standard Cards, Panels, & Inputs**: Employ a refined radius of `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px), delivering structured composure that aligns with hairline divider grids.
- **Hero Framing & Imagery**: Large imagery cards scale up to `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii to evoke tactile recipe tablets and natural stones.
- **Pills**: Badges, status tags, macronutrient chips, and primary action buttons utilize full capsule forms (`rounded-full` / 9999px), providing an ergonomic, tactile contrast against the structured editorial typography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), clad in Deep Jungle Green (`#12372A`) with Warm Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) text. Minimum height of 48px, horizontal padding of 28px. Typography is `label-lg`. On hover, shifts smoothly to Botanical Green (`#2D6A4F`) with an ambient green-tinted shadow.
- **Secondary (Architectural Outline)**: Pill or 8px rounded container with a 1px border of `#12372A`, transparent background, and `#12372A` typography. Hover transitions to `#F4EFEA`.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Link**: Text-only, featuring `Playfair Display` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` in `#12372A` paired with an animated 1px underline that expands from center.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutritional / Ingredient Pills**: Full capsule shape, background `#F4EFEA`, border 1px solid `#E8E3DC`, typography in `label-md` (`#1B4332`).
- **Accent Badges**: Soft Gold (`#D4AF37`) or Muted Terracotta (`#C86D51`) rendered at 10% background opacity with 100% solid color text and borders for dietary indicators (e.g., *Paleo*, *Keto*, *Anti-Inflammatory*).

### Cards & Culinary Containers
- **Recipe & Meal Plan Cards**: Constructed with `#FBF9F5` or `#F4EFEA` surface fill, wrapped in a 1px `#E8E3DC` border. Photography inside cards sits either flush to the top with an internal bleed or is framed by an intentional 12px margin mirroring framed gallery art. Card footers use subtle hairline dividers to partition prep time, calories, and macro distributions.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Text Fields**: Soft Cream (`#F4EFEA`) background with a 1px `#E8E3DC` hairline border, 12px vertical padding, and 16px horizontal padding. Border transitions to `#2D6A4F` on focus with no glowing outer ring, preserving an understated, quiet tactile feel. Placeholder text sits in `#637069`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and softly rounded square selectors using `#12372A` as the active check fill. Unselected states feature a 1.5px border of `#DFD7CC` over `#FBF9F5`. Checkmark glyphs are rendered in Warm Ivory (`#FBF9F5`).

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Macronutrient Architecture Bars**: Thin, elegant 4px horizontal micro-bars rendered on `#E8E3DC` tracks using Botanical Green (`#2D6A4F`), Warm Amber (`#D97706`), and Terracotta (`#C86D51`) for protein, carbohydrate, and healthy lipid balance.
- **Editorial Nutrition Callout**: A full-bleed quote module set in italicized `Playfair Display` atop an inverse `#12372A` background, accented by a single hairline divider and micro-metadata in Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`).
